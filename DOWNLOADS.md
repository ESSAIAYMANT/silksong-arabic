## Code signing policy

[Code signing policy](CODE_SIGNING.md). SignPath Foundation eligibility is under preparation, **not approved**. No code-signing provider endorsement is claimed.

A future installable release will have a signed installer, a checksum file, matched source, dependency notices, installation/removal instructions, supported game fingerprints and a hash-bound acceptance report. Installer tests and signing alone are not a guarantee of complete gameplay correctness.
